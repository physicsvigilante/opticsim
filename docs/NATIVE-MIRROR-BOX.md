# Native rectangular mirror box

Select `native-rectangular-mirror-box` explicitly to author a v35 scene with one SingleRay, four finite PhyDemo Mirror segments and one opaque PhyDemo Blocker inside. Give the coordinates, launch direction, brightness and segment dimensions. The rectangle spans 40–1000 mm on each axis. Center each axis-aligned mirror on its edge and leave a 0.1–2 mm gap at each end. The source and entire 2–20 mm axis-aligned target need at least 1 mm of clearance inside the rectangle. The perpendicular distance from the source to the target's supporting line must also be at least 1 mm.

An accepted run must record one native reflection on every mirror before a real `isAbsorbed:true` callback on the target. PhyDemo chooses the encounter order. The audit checks one continuous native ray, each incident point and reflected direction, both brightness channels and the final callback counts. Each mirror hit needs more than 1 mm of endpoint clearance and the target hit needs more than 0.1 mm, each with a 10⁻⁷ mm numerical reserve. All encounters need an absolute normal direction component of at least 0.02. Misses through corner gaps, grazing, early absorption, repeated reflections, warnings, stops and truncation withhold result claims.

The public observations are the existing `mirror-reflections` and `blocker-absorptions` counts by stable component ID. The numeric examples and controls are synthetic. The A04 source has no dimensions, so its conditional unfolding remains available separately.
