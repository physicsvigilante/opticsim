# Finite incidence into a native graded slab

The `native-entering-graded-slab` model uses schema v33. Give one single-ray source, one `graded-index-rectangle` and one passive detector. The rectangle starts at the authored `axisY` and extends upward by 0.2–0.4 mm with width 2–5 mm. Its squared refractive index is 1 + (y − axisY)/(1 mm), with unit-index exterior. Partial reflection is disabled. The source starts 0.1 mm below the near face, pointing 10–70° upward from positive x. The horizontal detector is 0.2 mm beyond the far face. Both slab contacts and the detector prediction must have at least 0.1 mm of lateral clearance.

Pinned PhyDemo traces the actual ray through the entrance, its native Euler steps, the far boundary and the detector. The profile fixes a 0.00025 mm step. Its own depth and processed budgets admit the several thousand native callbacks. The audit checks source identity, continuity, the sampled path and the far-face and detector contacts. It compares coordinates and outgoing direction against the conserved-momentum solution with a bounded tolerance. Stopped, ambiguous or out-of-tolerance execution withholds all accepted measurements. The tolerance is a criterion for the observed samples and geometry in this profile.

The example's finite incidence demonstrates the type of path in A15. The exact inward grazing limit and the critical direction at the far face are separate conditional calculations.
