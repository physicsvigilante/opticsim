# A directional fan, parallel slab and spherical mirror

The explicit model `native-fan-slab-arc-return-tracing` selects schema v31. In increasing x, specify one rightward directional point source, two slab faces, one vertical passive detector and one left-facing concave circular mirror. Supply the two absolute media indices, with one common exterior, a higher slab index and an index ratio no greater than 1.6. Both interfaces have normal 0° and partial reflection disabled.

The source uses a 1.5–2° directional cone and density 0.95–1. Its relative brightness may not exceed the density. The mirror radius is 100–400 mm and its aperture height is 40–100 mm. The source lies within 5 mm of the mirror axis and within 450 mm axially of the vertex. The detector is centred on the axis, with length 40–200 mm. Each successive element is at least 1 mm from its neighbour, measured to the nearest part of the finite arc. Use the dense authoring preset.

Each qualified sample has real native callbacks at both slab interfaces and the detector in both directions, and one intervening reflection. The adapter checks continuity between these encounters, local refraction and reflection, and endpoint clearance. A missed or ambiguous contact, incomplete run or inconsistent callback withholds the observations. The detector hit request includes directions. A negative x component identifies the returning samples. A crossing count includes both directions, and the signed power adds their signed contributions.

The A12 example states the assumed slab position, off-axis probe height, angular cone, relative brightness, mirror aperture and detector. Actual detector lines provide a finite geometric check on the returning bundle. The original question asks for image position and size changes. These are analytical quantities, while this contract publishes sampled native encounters and positions.
