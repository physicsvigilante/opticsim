# Focus and angular gain for two separated thin lenses

Use `two-lens-angular-focus` in the public problem workflow for two positive coaxial thin lenses in air and explicit collimated output. Distances use one declared unit (mm, cm or m). The objective stays fixed and the eyepiece is downstream. The signed gain compares paraxial slopes. Both focal lengths, stable element IDs and the unaided viewing reference are required.

[Microscope A39](../examples/angular-focus-a39.json) declares its 160 mm tube length as lens-plane separation. [Telescope A40](../examples/angular-focus-a40.json) gives object distance from the objective and assumes a relaxed eye at both adjustments. Run either through `node scripts/run-problem.mjs <example> --out test-artifacts/llm-authoring/<fresh-directory>`.

Let F and f denote the positive objective and eyepiece focal lengths and d the spacing. For collimated output the real intermediate image must lie f before the eyepiece. Its distance from the objective is v = d − f. Define Δ = d − F − f. For a finite real object at distance u > F, Δ = F²/(u − F); conversely u = F + F²/Δ. A mechanical tube length supplies d. An optical focal-plane interval supplies Δ. Input at infinity is expressed explicitly as `"infinity"` for object distance and gives d = F + f.

The signed angular gain for an unaided angle defined at the objective is −v/f. For an eye in the eyepiece plane, a finite object's unaided angle is smaller and the gain acquires a factor (u+d)/u. At infinity both references give −F/f. For an explicit finite comparison distance D the gain is −(Δ/F)(D/f). A39 therefore has magnitude 327.5 for the mechanical interpretation and 400 for the optical-interval interpretation. A40 requires an outward shift of 1/38 m on moving from infinity focus to the 10 m object. Its gain is −10 at infinity and −200/19 at 10 m with an objective reference.

Finite input lengths are bounded to 0.001–1,000,000 metres. Real intermediate images and positive optical intervals are required for finite-object adjustment. Near-singular differences and out-of-range conjugates withhold results. The reduced query returns analytical evidence. The [frozen protocol](../evaluation/separated-lens-angular-focus-v1/frozen-protocol.json) and separate native controls use actual separated IdealLens instances and detector directions. These samples do not establish pupil throughput or clinical vision.
