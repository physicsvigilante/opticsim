# An immersed concave spherical mirror with a planar exit

Set `physical.model` to `native-immersed-arc-plane-port` to select v34. The dense preset samples one axial directional source in a denser homogeneous half-space. A native planar interface separates the exterior to the left. A finite native concave ArcMirror faces left inside the denser medium. One vertical detector is outside the plane and centred on the same axis.

Give both refractive indices, a positive x interface normal and partial reflection disabled. The index ratio is at most 1.6. Use a mirror radius of 50–250 mm and a finite aperture of 30–100 mm no larger than its radius. The mirror pole is at most 300 mm from the plane. The source is at least 1 mm from the plane and the nearest part of the mirror. Its cone opening is 1.5–2°, pointed right toward the mirror or left toward the exit. Detector length is 30–100 mm and its clearance from the plane is 1–100 mm.

Point the source right to qualify a reflection and then passage through the plane. Point it left to qualify direct exit. Each orientation runs independently. The adapter checks actual native callbacks and withholds accepted results if any emitted ray is missing, takes an inconsistent branch or falls into the endpoint clearance band. A mirror hit must lie on the authored finite arc on the pole side of the supporting circle. The audit requires the emitted brightness of each polarization channel to persist through the interfaces and mirror. It checks the passive detector's signed power from a zero baseline through the last reading. Requests can select the mirror count, interface encounters and exterior detector hit events.

A28 motivates the bounded mirror patch and plane. Refractive material on the right is an explicit half-space. The patch samples the near-axis reflected branch of the silvered surface. Axial conjugate positions from the paraxial model are available in a separate query.
