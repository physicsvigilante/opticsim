## Public admission

**Isolated schema 22**, optical model `native-parallel-prism-tracing`. Exactly one actual `Beam`, exactly one `triangular-prism` compiled into native `Glass`, and 0–2 passive downstream vertical `Detector` segments. No other source, mirror, lens, stop, interface or nested medium. Neither full prism inverse nor symbolic multi-path closure is implied. No older schema is broadened or migrated.

Required author-controlled triangle vertices `(x1,y1),(x2,y2),(x3,y3)`: `x1===x2`, `y2>y1`, `x3>=x1+1`, `y1+1<y3<y2-1`. This defines the actual flat vertical face 12 as the intended initial entrance surface; 23 and 31 are real sloping native faces. Every finite edge and altitude must be at least 1 mm, all derived endpoints within +/-10,000 mm. **Only initial** horizontal rightward Beam with explicit x/y, 10–100 mm transverse width, brightness <= ray density (.048–1) and at most 100 emitted native samples. The **whole emitting segment** is behind the flat face and strictly more than 0.1 mm from its two endpoints. The native emitted sample lines are screened for a forward vertex within 0.1 mm, including the physical apex. No outgoing Snell/reflection ray is guessed by the validator.

Medium absolute indices `insideIndex` and `outsideIndex` between 1 and 4 must be explicit, with inside>outside. `partialReflection=false` is an **explicit transmitted-only Fresnel approximation**, not native TIR disablement. Real TIR and return paths remain native, including the genuine case where **zero** rays cross a right-hand detector. Downstream passive Detectors are finite vertical segments entirely to the right of the actual triangle apex, separated from all surfaces and each other by at least 0.01 mm. All ray detector counts are sampled callbacks, not continuous flux; no optical image is inferred.

Every actual native Glass callback is observed at its reported point. **Historical v8 retains the original strictly-less-than 0.01 mm vertex rule**. New v22 uses a distinct, inclusive **0.1 mm vertex exclusion around every actual later Glass hit**, in addition to its 0.1 mm initial straight-line sample guard. A v22-native incidence with `n_incident * |sin(theta_incident)|` within **1e-5 (dimensionless)** of `n_destination` is withheld as a numerically near-critical interface, regardless of whether pinned native reports transmission, TIR or a completion callback. These checks examine actual native incident directions/normals after the callback is selected, never predict or reroute any optical branch. Unknown, grazing, undefined, truncated, stopped and stale results likewise withhold all qualified requested measurements. The old v8 event contract is not tightened or migrated. Returned outgoing path is never altered or fabricated. Detector hit positions require existing event-cap, endpoint clearance and count matching, while the aggregate true native crossing count can remain valid even if a bounded coordinate list is withheld.

## Boundaries and test plan

The JEE J13/J14 source text does not supply this synthetic triangle's dimensions, exterior index, finite Beam width/brightness or downstream Detector; the public example explicitly declares them as *teaching controls*, not original givens or a verified original answer. The example demonstrates multi-face native interaction and a genuine zero right-hand detector count; a separately authored small apex-to-base depth demonstrates positive finite refracted detector intersections, if native confirmed. No second prism, general Beam+Glass composition, special inverse solver, 3D volume, image/visibility claim, diffraction or arbitrary path identity is promised.

Tests must cover real native face events/TIR, separate first-event/face counts, genuine positive and zero downstream hits, index and density/width/translation edits, explicit rejection of missing inputs and vertex risks, incomplete/stopped/stale suppression, historical v8/v19/v20/v21 schema isolation, public problem-to-native qualified export, and real Chrome edit/invalid/save/reopen. The final exact source tree and all CI evidence require **ONE independent combined physics/integration review** before a separate owner merge decision. The research PR #172 stays draft/unmerged; no automatic merge or deployment.
