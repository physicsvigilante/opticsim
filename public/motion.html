Assumptions, limits and provenance

Loading…

Native completion is not image detection. The camera fits finite sampled image positions without changing physics. Infinite, indeterminate or extreme out-of-range positions have no finite guide. A dashed marker is an analytical guide, not a natively detected image. Circular-mirror rays may exhibit aberration; their single image prediction is paraxial. A stopped or failed native frame is never labelled complete.

Static scene export is one snapshot. The experiment file preserves the motion.
